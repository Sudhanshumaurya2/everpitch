Why webinars

Why a focused presentation still works

A sales page gets a quick scan. A webinar gets attention for long enough to explain what you do and why it matters. That is especially useful for services, software and new ideas, which are hard to sell in bullet points.

Webinars also build trust before the ask: viewers hear the reasoning, see the demo and get their questions answered. The difficulty has always been the production work. That is the part EverPitch is designed to remove.
