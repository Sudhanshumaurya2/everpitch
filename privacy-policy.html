Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

What we collect

This website has no user accounts, contact forms or newsletter sign-up, and it does not ask for personal information.

Browser storage

To avoid showing the promotional pop-up repeatedly, the site stores a timestamp in your browser’s local storage, and remembers for the current session if you dismiss the notification card. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Hosting logs

Our hosting provider (Vercel) may process technical data such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure the website, under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

When you click a purchase button, you are forwarded to the seller’s checkout on JVZoo. Any information you enter there is handled by the seller and JVZoo under their policies, not by this website.

Changes

If we add analytics or other data collection, we will update this page first.
