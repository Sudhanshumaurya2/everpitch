EverPitch FAQ: Pricing, Credits, Refunds and Setup

Answers to the questions people ask most about EverPitch and the Bundle, based on the seller’s published information.

The Bundle

What is EverPitch?

EverPitch is AI webinar software. You give it a topic, a document, a link or a recording, and it creates the script, the slides and the voice-over. Once you have reviewed it, EverPitch presents the webinar on a schedule and answers viewers’ questions out loud. See the product overview.

What is included in the EverPitch Bundle?

EverPitch Commercial, DFY Webinars, Kit Vault and Agency, with 8,000 credits a month and three bonuses: The First 7 Days Playbook, The Webinar Title & Hook Bank and The After-Webinar Email Pack.

How much does the Bundle cost?

The seller lists the Bundle at $199.10 as a one-time payment, with no yearly renewal. Bought separately, the seller lists the first-year total at $307.10. Always check the final price at checkout.

Is Ultra included?

No. According to the seller, Ultra (streaming, MP4 export, host on camera and voice calls) is sold separately. Done-for-you webinars are already part of the Bundle.

Can I buy EverPitch on its own?

Yes. The seller offers EverPitch on its own as a one-time payment, with upgrades available after checkout. This site links to the Bundle offer.

Getting started

Do I need technical skills?

No. You describe your webinar in plain words, pick a kit, or upload something you already have. There is nothing to install. The How It Works page walks through the three steps.

How do I get my login after buying?

Right after checkout the seller emails you a link to set up your EverPitch account. You choose a password and everything in the Bundle is already switched on.

Do I have to appear on camera?

No. EverPitch writes, designs and voices the whole webinar, so you never have to record yourself. If you want to, you can add on-camera clips of yourself made from one photo.

Credits

What are credits used for?

Credits pay for building webinars, at 40 credits per finished minute. A 15-minute webinar uses about 600. Extra languages and extra AI images use a few more.

How many credits does the Bundle include?

8,000 credits a month (about 200 finished minutes), compared with 3,000 for EverPitch on its own. Credits refresh every month.

What happens if I run out of credits?

Webinars you have already built keep running. New builds wait until your credits refresh the next month.

Do viewer questions cost credits?

No. Spoken answers during a session are included, up to 20 answers per session.

Using it for your business

Is an EverPitch webinar really live?

The talk is prepared in advance and played on a shared clock, so everyone in a session sees the same moment. Questions are answered on the spot from your content. A simulated audience is optional, and when it is switched on, your registration page says so.

What if the AI host gets a fact wrong?

Answers come only from what is in your webinar, and you can read and edit every line of the script before going live. Spam and abuse in the chat are screened before anything is read out.

Can I use EverPitch for client work?

Every account can add its own logo, name and colour. Agency, included in the Bundle, adds the licence to make and run webinars for clients and removes “Powered by EverPitch” from the pages. Read more in Benefits.

Can I clone someone’s voice?

Only with their consent. When you clone a voice from a recording, you confirm that the speaker has agreed. Answers are then spoken in that voice.
