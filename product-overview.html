What Is EverPitch? The AI Webinar Builder Explained

EverPitch is cloud software that turns your knowledge and your offer into a complete webinar, then presents it for you every day. Here is what it is, what it makes, and what the Bundle adds.

Get the EverPitch Bundle →

The short version

Creating a webinar normally means a stack of jobs: planning the pitch, writing the script, building slides, preparing demos, rehearsing, presenting, handling questions and preparing the replay. EverPitch is built to take on each of those jobs.

You start from whatever you have: a one-line idea, a document, a web page, a PDF deck or an old Zoom recording. EverPitch drafts the talk, designs the slides and narrates them in a natural AI voice. You review and edit everything. Then EverPitch hosts it on a schedule and answers viewers’ questions out loud, using only your content.

Core features

What every EverPitch account includes

AI-written webinar scripts

A structured talk with an opening, the teaching and a natural move to your offer, drafted from your topic or material.

AI-designed slides

73 slide layouts and 11 visual styles, with AI slide images, your logo and brand colours, and PDF deck import.

Natural AI narration

30 AI voices across 87 languages, so you never need to record yourself or appear on camera.

Automated daily hosting

The webinar runs on flexible schedules, shown in each viewer’s local timezone, on a shared clock.

Pages and emails included

The Bundle

Four products, already switched on

The EverPitch Bundle adds more credits, done-for-you webinars, ready-made kits and client rights, all active from your first login.

More credits: 8,000 a month

Up from 3,000, or about 200 finished minutes. That is enough for roughly 13 fifteen-minute webinars a month. Credits refresh monthly.

Kit Vault: 24 webinar kits

Ready-made kits for coaching, courses, SaaS, agencies and local services. Each sets the structure, length and style; you fill in the marked blanks and build.

Agency: a client licence

Make, run and charge for webinars for clients, with their logo, name and colour, and no “Powered by EverPitch” on registration, live room or replay pages.

DFY Webinars: 10 finished webinars

About 15 minutes each, designed and voiced, each with its registration page, emails, poll and offer card. They are neutral so your offer fits, and copying them uses no credits.

  • The First 7 Days Playbook: one small job a day, from first login to a live, promoted webinar.

Who it suits

Built for experts who don’t want to become producers

The seller positions EverPitch for people who know their subject and their offer but don’t have time to script, design, record and present: coaches, consultants, course creators, local service businesses, SaaS teams and agencies.

  • You explain the same offer repeatedly on calls
  • You sell something that needs explaining, like a service, software or a new idea
  • You’d rather not appear on camera
  • You build marketing assets for clients

It suits you less if you need live streaming, MP4 export or an on-camera host in real time. Those are part of Ultra, which is sold separately.
