Questions before you order

What is EverPitch?

EverPitch is AI webinar software. You give it a topic, a document, a link or a recording, and it creates the script, the slides and the voice-over. Once you have reviewed it, EverPitch presents the webinar on a schedule and answers viewers’ questions out loud. See the product overview.

What is included in the EverPitch Bundle?

EverPitch Commercial, DFY Webinars, Kit Vault and Agency, with 8,000 credits a month and three bonuses: The First 7 Days Playbook, The Webinar Title & Hook Bank and The After-Webinar Email Pack.

How much does the Bundle cost?

The seller lists the Bundle at $199.10 as a one-time payment, with no yearly renewal. Bought separately, the seller lists the first-year total at $307.10. Always check the final price at checkout.

Is Ultra included?

No. According to the seller, Ultra (streaming, MP4 export, host on camera and voice calls) is sold separately. Done-for-you webinars are already part of the Bundle.

Can I buy EverPitch on its own?

Yes. The seller offers EverPitch on its own as a one-time payment, with upgrades available after checkout. This site links to the Bundle offer.

Do I need technical skills?

No. You describe your webinar in plain words, pick a kit, or upload something you already have. There is nothing to install. The How It Works page walks through the three steps.

How do I get my login after buying?

Right after checkout the seller emails you a link to set up your EverPitch account. You choose a password and everything in the Bundle is already switched on.

Do I have to appear on camera?

No. EverPitch writes, designs and voices the whole webinar, so you never have to record yourself. If you want to, you can add on-camera clips of yourself made from one photo.
